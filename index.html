Privacy Notice & Policy

This Privacy Notice outlines how Stock Market collects, handles, and protects information submitted through this trader qualification assessment.

1. Information Collected

  • Assessment Details: Self-reported trading challenge, trading segment, and capital range.
  • Contact Information: Full name, primary mobile number, WhatsApp number, and state of residence.
  • Technical Metadata: IP address, user-agent details, and marketing campaign attribution parameters (UTM tags).

2. Purpose of Collection

Information collected is used strictly to evaluate candidate suitability for trader qualification, schedule introductory consultation calls, communicate qualification updates via WhatsApp or mobile phone, and prevent automated abuse.

3. Data Security & Retention

Submitted data is securely stored in a managed PostgreSQL environment with encrypted transit (SSL/TLS). We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact information with unrelated third-party marketing brokers.
